How the board is built

The board holds every protocol in the snapshot that records no token — no gecko id, no symbol. The house watchlist (TVL at or above the floor, no token, not a wrapped or staked product of a token that trades) is marked with a ★; it is the floor of the board, never its ceiling, so protocols below the floor are ranked beside it. Protocols whose token already trades are not ranked here at all: they sit under Already trading, where they do the measuring.

The value model

For each pre-token protocol, the implied value of its future token is

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × reach

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap/TVL ratio of the snapshot’s own token-bearing protocols in the same category. Ratios are winsorised to [0.005, 10] so one mispriced token cannot bend a category, shrunk toward the global median with a prior weight of 4 samples, and clamped to [0.03, 4]. A category with no token-bearing sample uses the global median. Nothing is hardcoded: change the snapshot and every multiple moves.
  • Momentum — 1 + 0.35 · tanh(change7d / 20). Seven-day TVL growth marks a protocol up by up to a third; a fall marks it down the same way. No reported change is neutral.
  • Maturity — 1 + 0.5 · (1 − e−age/540), age being the days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol. The longer a protocol has run at size with no token, the more product stands behind the eventual launch. No listing date is neutral.
  • Reach — 1 + 0.16 · ln(chains), capped at +45%. A protocol already live on many chains gives a token more surface to be useful on.

Risk flags

Each row carries the flags its own figures earn: TVL down 15% or more in a week, momentum overheated at +40% or more, single-chain exposure, a crowded category (25 or more pre-token rivals), a young protocol (tracked under 180 days), or data the snapshot does not report (no 7-day change, no listing date). Flags add up to a Low / Medium / High label — a falling TVL counts double.

Farm plans

Every row’s plan is six steps written from a playbook for its category, filled with the protocol’s own chains: set up and fund the wallet, deploy a position sized to the protocol’s TVL, work it weekly, expand, track eligibility, and be ready on launch day — each step with what it costs in gas on that chain and how long it takes.
